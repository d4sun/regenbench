## GGUF Attack Surface (mirrors `docs/demo-report.md#5` when corpus not crawled)

`ggufref` (reference-parser oracle, `containers/gguf`) detects all 7 families; the pickle-oriented static panel (`modelscan`/`fickling`) does not:

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |

*`fickling` is pickle-specific; it treats GGUF bytes as unknown and historically returns `benign` or crashes. The `ggufref` oracle is the correct detector for this format (signature-driven for 6 malformed-header families + render-driven for SSTI via `jinja2.Environment`).
